# Airtkey's

## AirTkey's DIY DePIN Guides

Built and maintained by Alexander Gretz — Tech Ambassador & DePIN operator based in Bavaria, Germany.

***

#### About This Site

Airtkey.xyz is a resource hub for DePIN enthusiasts — covering hardware flashing, node setup, operations, and hands-on guides built from real-world experience.

#### About Me

I'm a hands-on builder with 15+ years of experience across print, media, NFC technology, and sustainable business — now fully focused on Web3 and DePIN.

My journey started in traditional print, moved into pioneering NFC payment systems at Schreiner Group — contributing to Mastercard's first contactless payment stickers — before smartphones even had NFC.

Today I operate DePIN nodes across multiple networks and serve as Tech Ambassador at onocoy — supporting community onboarding, evaluating node locations, and contributing to ecosystem strategy.
