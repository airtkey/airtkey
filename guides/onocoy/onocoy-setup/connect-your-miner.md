# Connect Your Miner

### Connect Your Miner

Once you have your NTRIP credentials, you can connect your hardware to the onocoy network.

👉 Official onocoy installation guide: [https://docs.onocoy.com/documentation/3.-become-a-miner/2.-install-your-station](https://docs.onocoy.com/documentation/3.-become-a-miner/2.-install-your-station)

#### Which build are you using?

👉 ESP32 + UM980 → See Build Guide A 👉 Raspberry Pi + Mosaic-X5 → See Build Guide B

#### After connecting, check your station:

1. Go to [https://console.onocoy.com](https://console.onocoy.com/)
2. Click "My Stations"
3. Your station should show as 🟢 Online
4. You can also find your station on the public explorer: [https://console.onocoy.com/explorer](https://console.onocoy.com/explorer)

> ✅ If your station shows green — congratulations! You are now mining ONO rewards! 🎉
