# Create an onocoy Account

### Create an onocoy Account

To get started, you'll need to register on the onocoy platform.

👉 Follow the official onocoy guide here: [https://docs.onocoy.com/documentation/3.-become-a-miner/3.-connect-your-station-to-onocoy](https://docs.onocoy.com/documentation/3.-become-a-miner/3.-connect-your-station-to-onocoy)

#### What you'll need:

* A valid email address
* A Solana wallet (we cover this in the next step)

#### Steps:

1. Go to [https://app.onocoy.com](https://app.onocoy.com/)
2. Click "Sign Up"
3. Enter your email and create a password
4. Verify your email address
5. Log in to your new account

> ✅ Done! Your account is ready. Next step: Set up your Solana wallet.
